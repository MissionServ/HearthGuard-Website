Take the Oath Hear the Tale

Long before contractors carried business cards, an order carried oaths.

When the first hearths were lit, a decay took root in every wall and roof — the slow siege of Wear and Rot, patient enough to outlast any single homeowner. Gutters choked with silence. Shingles gave way to weather no one was watching for. The order rose to answer it: sworn craftsmen who patrolled cottage and hall alike, mending what time undid before it could take hold.

The order scattered for an age — its Wardens scattered into a hundred trades, their oath forgotten by all but a few. But the oath was never broken.

Today the Hearthguard rides again: a guild of vetted Wardens, dispatched house to house, keeping the siege at bay one home at a time.

— From the Charter of the Hearthguard, Founding Chapter
